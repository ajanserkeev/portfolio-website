## Context & Challenge

An international freight forwarding enterprise tracked complex multi-leg shipments using spreadsheets and fragmented messaging groups. Freight brokers spent over 5 hours per deal transcribing invoices, verifying rates, and handling manual client tracking requests.

## Solution

We established a streamlined operational conveyor based on amoCRM and automated n8n workflows:

- **Automated intake:** parses incoming freight requests from email and messengers, extracting weights, dimensions, and waypoints.
- **Digital shipment passport:** generates pipeline deals with linked customs forms, CMR documents, and cost calculations.
- **Client notifications:** dispatches real-time customs clearance milestones and GPS tracking updates.
- **Accounting linkage:** 1-click invoice and receipt generation in enterprise ERP.

## Measurable Results

- Saved **4.5 hours** of manual coordinator time per shipment.
- Completely eliminated lost inquiries and transcription errors.
- Repeat client order volume increased by **22%**.
