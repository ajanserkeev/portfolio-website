## Context & Challenge

A major construction material distributor lost deals to agile competitors due to prolonged commercial proposal prep: sales managers manually confirmed availability across three distribution centers and calculated complex tiered discounts.

## Solution

We architected a unified sales and dispatch workflow:

- **Instant quote builder:** smart CRM configurator queries live warehouse stock and outputs customized PDF quotes with instant pay QR codes in 30 seconds.
- **Field rep mobile app:** Telegram WebApp enabling agents to place reserve orders on customer job sites with geo-verification.
- **Credit control triggers:** automated payment reminders and automatic dispatch holds when credit limits are exceeded.

## Measurable Results

- Reduced typical sales cycles by **40%**.
- Tripled repeat purchase cadence via targeted CRM re-engagement automation.
- Reduced shipping spec mismatches down to zero.
