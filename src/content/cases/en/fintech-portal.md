## Context & Challenge

A fintech provider sought to overhaul its legacy portal for corporate accounts: the slow monolith took over 6 seconds to load, suffered high drop-off rates during company document verification, and rendered poorly on mobile devices used by executive decision-makers.

## Solution

We engineered a cutting-edge hybrid web architecture using Astro and interactive islands:

- **Sub-second first paint:** pre-rendered marketing and documentation screens achieving LCP under 1.1s on cellular networks.
- **Interactive 3D dashboards:** Three.js data stream visualizations running smoothly at 60 FPS on mobile devices.
- **Guided KYC onboarding:** real-time corporate registry validation, biometric verification, and digital contract signing completed in 4 minutes.

## Measurable Results

- Full page load latency plunged from 6.2s to **1.1s**.
- Corporate onboarding completion speed jumped by **45%**.
- Achieved **99.98%** platform uptime through seasonal transaction peaks reaching 15,000 RPS.
