## Context & Challenge

A boutique resort comprising 28 chalets surrendered up to 25% in commission fees to online travel agencies. Their existing landing page featured a basic contact form where leads often lagged, resulting in missed dates and occasional double bookings.

## Solution

We developed a high-converting, lightweight booking engine:

- **Live availability calendar:** two-way iCal synchronization with major OTAs eliminates overbooking risk.
- **Interactive resort map:** guests visualize exact chalet locations, proximity to lake shores, and interior floor plans.
- **Frictionless deposit checkout:** embedded payment processing with instant voucher issuance.
- **Staff mobile alerts:** front desk staff receive instant booking profiles with customer arrival notes in Telegram.

## Measurable Results

- Direct zero-commission bookings surged by **34%**.
- Guests finalize their stay in an average of **40 seconds**.
- Zero double-booking incidents across two full operating seasons.
